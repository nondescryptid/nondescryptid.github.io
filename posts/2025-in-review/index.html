3 different varieties of sunscreen used#

All of them are SPF50 and PA++++ (but PPD varies - PA++++ only means “this has a PPD of at least 16”) Ranked in order from “skin-like finish” to “kinda dewy”:

  1. Haru Haru Wonder Black Rice Moisture Airyfit Daily Sunscreen
  2. Numbuzin No. 1 Clear Filter Sunscreen
  3. P.Calm Water Barrier Sunscreen

I found all of them decent for Singapore’s humid weather, but prefer (2) and (3) simply because they have a simple tube packaging instead of a tube + a pump (which feels a bit excessive for something that’s just 50ml).
